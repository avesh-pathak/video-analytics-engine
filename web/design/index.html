01 / Principles

Soft technology, serious utility.

Seven rules keep a hundred-model product feeling like one calm app.

01

One subscription, one surface

Every tool is shown inside the same rounded white app window. The UI is the proof that 100+ models and six tools have been consolidated.

02

Soft technology

Mint, sky and lilac replace the dark “AI” aesthetic. No neon, no black backgrounds, no glitch.

03

Pill = action

Anything that commits is a full-radius pill. Anything informational is a large rounded rectangle.

04

Abundance, calmly

100+ models as a slow, edge-faded marquee of small chips. Dense, but never loud.

05

Proof by faces and numbers

YC badge, 1M+ users, 15K+ reviews, avatar cluster, star ratings, named testimonials.

06

Motion you feel

420–640ms vertical slides, 2.2s halos, 175s+ marquees. Nothing spins or flashes.

07

Mobile-first commerce

A persistent Monthly / Yearly / Buy Now bar appears on phones. Yearly is the visual default, and the yearly plan is the visual hero on desktop too.

HERO CHOREOGRAPHY · MEASURED FROM FRAMES

Six icons. One window. A vertical stage.

Selecting a tool does not swap the whole panel. The label, headline and product window slide vertically as one stage: old content exits upward, new content enters from below, over 640ms with cubic-bezier(.22,.7,.28,1). The gradient panel stays fixed, so the page never jumps. The active icon becomes a mint pill with a 4px white ring and a tray notch pointing at the window.
